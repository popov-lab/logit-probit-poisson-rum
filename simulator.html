Numbers behind the charts

Model. Counters Xi(t) share a Poisson stream of rate λ₀ (increment +1 to all) and, for each pair (i, j), two opposed streams with rates uij ± dij/(2√θ) and loadings ±(ei − ej), where dij = (vi − vj)/K. As θ → ∞ the winner converges to argmaxi (ṽi + εi) with ε ~ N(0, Σcontrast), Σcontrast = 2λ₀ Σ uij(ei−ej)(ei−ej)ᵀ — a weighted graph Laplacian. For K = 2 this gives ρ = (λ₀ − 2u)/(λ₀ + 2u) and P(1) → Φ(a/√(2λ₀u)) with a = (v₁−v₂)/2. Rate positivity requires |dij| < 2uij√θ: expressible utility contrasts are bounded by available noise at finite θ.
