Numbers behind the charts

Model (Result 5). For any target utilities v and covariance Σ, only the choice-relevant parts ṽ = Pv and Σc = PΣP matter, with P = I − 11ᵀ/K. Writing Σc = Σ qr wrwrᵀ, the race uses a shared stream of rate λ₀ (loading 1) and, per eigendirection, paired streams with loadings ±wr and rates ur ± dr/(2√θ), where ur = qr/2λ₀ and dr = wrᵀv. As θ → ∞ the winner converges to argmaxi(vi + εi), ε ~ N(0, Σ), up to choice-irrelevant common components. For a null direction (qr = 0, dr ≠ 0) the rate ur,θ ∝ θ−1/4 vanishes while the drift survives. Rate positivity requires |dr| < 2ur√θ: expressible utility contrasts are bounded by available noise at finite θ. Unlike pairwise reciprocal inhibition — whose contrast covariances are weighted graph Laplacians (nonpositive off-diagonals) — general signed loadings reach every choice-relevant probit covariance.
